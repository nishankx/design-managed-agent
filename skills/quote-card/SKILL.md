# Quote Card

The quote is the design. Everything else stays quiet. Default format: `square` (1080x1080).

## Getting the quote

- Use the quote **exactly** as given, including its punctuation. Never shorten or reword it.
- Always credit the person: their name, plus their role or where they said it, if you have it.
- If the quote is longer than about 25 words, ask the user which part to use.

Colors and fonts below are **roles**. Look up the actual hex values and font
names in `brand-guidelines`.

## Layout

Use the **primary** color as the background, so the accent stays readable.

1. **Opening mark:** a large quotation mark (“) in **accent**, heading font, weight 700, about 200px, near the top left.
2. **The quote:** heading font, weight 600, 48 to 64px, in **surface** or **background**, starting about a third of the way down. Break it across 4 to 6 lines.
3. **Attribution:** body font, weight 600, 30px, in **accent**, starting with a dash, about 80px below the quote.
4. **Role or source** (optional): body font, weight 400, 26px, in **surface** or **background**, just under the attribution.

Keep a 96px margin on every side, and align everything to the left margin.

## Checks before you call it done

- The quote matches what the user gave you, word for word.
- No line of the quote is wider than the canvas minus its margins.
